# Demo script (2–3 minutes, screen recording with voiceover)

**0:00 – 0:15 · Hook.** Welcome screen. "This is a garden of sound seeds. Every seed is a qubit. You will hear
superposition, entanglement and interference before you ever see a gate." Press *Enter the garden* (sound starts).

**0:15 – 0:50 · Make It Rain (challenge 1).** Press Bloom with no actions: hush. Predict "Always rain", press Flip,
Bloom: rain on every shot. Show the reveal: "Flip is the X gate" and the Qiskit code. One sentence: "The sound is
driven by real Qiskit measurements, and the app says which engine produced them."

**0:50 – 1:35 · Perfect Pair (challenge 2).** Split the sky, Link sky to storm. Predict "always together or neither".
Bloom: rain+thunder or hush, never thunder alone. Then *Go deeper*: "Classical twins, sideways" — thunder on dry
nights appears. "Your pair, sideways" — still together or neither. "That is entanglement: agreement that survives a
different question."

**1:35 – 2:15 · Silence the Footsteps (challenge 3).** Twist: nothing changes (phase is invisible). Split: footsteps
every time. Twist half a turn, then Split: silence. "Interference. The paths remembered each other." Optional:
*Dephased twin* brings the footsteps back.

**2:15 – 2:40 · Repair the Garden (challenge 4), fast.** Show the tangled six-action garden blooming with stray
thunder, remove the redundant pairs, bloom again: match jumps. "Fewer gates, less noise."

**2:40 – 3:00 · Creator + close.** Assign sounds to 00/01/10/11, two or three actions, Bloom: a 20-second
soundscape with predicted-versus-measured bars and the Qiskit code. End on the backend pill (Qollab backend or
IonQ simulator) and the line "This effort is supported by Qollab & IonQ."

Tips: use *Skip to the result* to keep the video tight; keep *Show gate names* on for the second half.
