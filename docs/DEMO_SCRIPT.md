# Demo script (2–3 minutes, screen recording with voiceover)

**0:00 – 0:15 · Hook.** Welcome screen. "Four moves. Every garden you grow is a real quantum circuit." Press
*Learn the moves* (sound starts).

**0:15 – 0:40 · The moves.** The rulebook: press *Hear it* on **Split** (a coin toss), then on **Twist, then Split**
(rain every time). "You just heard interference, before seeing a single gate." Press *Start the puzzles*.

**0:40 – 1:00 · Make It Rain (puzzle 1).** The board: Flip, Split, Twist, Bloom. Press Flip, press Bloom; it asks
"what will you hear?", choose *Always rain*: rain on every shot. Press *Why?*: "Flip is the X gate" and the Qiskit
code. One sentence: "The sound is driven by real Qiskit measurements, and the app says which engine produced them."

**1:00 – 1:40 · Perfect Pair (puzzle 2).** Split the sky, Link sky to storm, Bloom, choose "always together, or
neither": rain+thunder or hush, never thunder alone. Press *Why?*, then *Go deeper*: "Classical twins, sideways" —
thunder on dry nights appears. "Your pair, sideways" — still together or neither. "That is entanglement: agreement
that survives a different question."

**1:40 – 2:15 · Silence the Footsteps (puzzle 3).** Twist: nothing changes (phase is invisible). Split: footsteps
every time. Twist half a turn, then Split: silence. "Interference. The paths remembered each other." Optional:
*Dephased twin* brings the footsteps back.

**2:15 – 2:35 · Repair the Garden (puzzle 4), fast.** The tangled six-move garden blooms with stray thunder; tap the
redundant beads away, Bloom again: the match jumps. "Fewer gates, less noise."

**2:35 – 3:00 · Creator + close.** Four pads, a sound each; two or three moves; Bloom: a 20-second soundscape. Press
*Circuit* in the top bar: bits, predicted-versus-measured bars and the Qiskit code appear. End on the backend pill
(Qollab backend or IonQ simulator) and the line "This effort is supported by Qollab & IonQ."

Tips: *Skip* keeps the video tight; turn *Circuit* on for the second half so judges see the gates and the code.
